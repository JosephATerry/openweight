# Customer Data and Privacy Access Policy

## Purpose and classification

This fictional policy governs access to customer account records, support conversations, usage events, authentication history, and other customer-linked information. Customer data is confidential and must be used only for an approved support, security, product-operation, legal, or analytics purpose.

## Minimum necessary access

Requests must name the dataset or application, the customer population, the fields needed, the intended use, and the requested duration. The data owner must confirm that the purpose is compatible with customer notices and contractual commitments. Production access is time-bound by default. Analysts should use aggregated or de-identified datasets whenever individual records are not required. Support personnel may access an account only while handling a linked support case.

Access requires a managed identity, multi-factor authentication, current privacy training, and an approved ticket. Highly sensitive fields such as authentication secrets, payment details, government identifiers, and precise location require additional approval from Privacy or Security. A broad customer-data role must not be granted merely because a narrower view is inconvenient.

## Use, export, and retention

Customer data must remain in approved systems. Screenshots, local spreadsheets, personal notebooks, and unapproved collaboration tools are prohibited. Any export must follow the Data Export and Transfer Policy, including recipient validation, encryption, minimum fields, retention, and deletion evidence. Customer records must not be copied into development or model-training datasets without a separately approved data-use review.

## Audit and incident handling

Systems must log searches, record views, exports, permission changes, and administrative actions. Data owners review access quarterly and after role changes. Unusual browsing, bulk queries, or access without a linked business record must be investigated.

During a suspected customer-data incident, responders may receive narrowly scoped emergency access under the Incident Response Access Policy. Incident status does not automatically permit data export or disclosure to a vendor. Privacy and Legal must review any notification or exceptional use.
