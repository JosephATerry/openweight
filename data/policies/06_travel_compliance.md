# Business Travel and Compliance Policy

## Purpose and authorization

This fictional policy governs Organization-funded travel and the protection of systems and information while traveling. Travel must have a documented business purpose, manager approval, and a cost center before booking. Travelers must use the approved travel service when available and choose reasonable transport and lodging consistent with safety requirements.

Travel to a location identified by Security or Compliance as elevated risk requires pre-travel review. The traveler must disclose the countries of transit, expected work activities, equipment carried, and whether customer, employee, source-code, encryption, or export-controlled information may be accessed. Legal and Compliance determine whether licenses, customer consent, or activity restrictions apply.

## Devices and access

Only managed devices may access Organization systems during travel. Multi-factor authentication remains mandatory. Security may issue a temporary travel device with limited data and time-bound access. Travelers must not use shared hotel computers, public charging data ports, or unknown removable media. Sensitive conversations and screen content must be protected from observation.

Downloading customer records, payroll files, source repositories, or privileged credentials for offline convenience is prohibited. If business work requires a data export, the traveler must follow the Data Export and Transfer Policy before departure. Border inspection, device loss, unexpected detention, or suspected account compromise must be reported immediately through the incident channel.

## Expenses and prohibited conduct

Receipts and a clear business description are required under the expense standard. Personal upgrades, companion costs, fines, and unapproved entertainment are not reimbursable. Gifts, hospitality, or facilitation payments intended to influence an official or business decision are prohibited. Travelers must follow anti-bribery, sanctions, and local conduct requirements.

Exceptions require written approval before travel unless a documented safety emergency makes advance approval impossible. Retrospective expense approval does not cure a data-access, export, sanctions, or security violation.
