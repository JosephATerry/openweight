# Procurement Approval and Purchasing Policy

## Purpose and purchasing channels

This fictional policy establishes approval and documentation rules for purchasing software, services, equipment, and professional work. Purchases must use the approved procurement system and an authorized supplier record. Employees must not commit the Organization through email, click-through terms, purchase-card splitting, or verbal promises before required reviews are complete.

## Request content and approvals

A purchase request must state the business purpose, cost center, total expected commitment, term, renewal terms, supplier, alternatives considered, and accountable owner. The requester must disclose conflicts of interest and must not approve their own request. Budget-owner approval is required for every purchase. Higher-value or multi-year commitments require Finance approval under the current delegation schedule.

Security review is required when a supplier will connect to Organization systems, process confidential data, provide hosted software, or receive credentials. Privacy review is required for customer or employee information. Legal review is required for non-standard terms, regulated activity, intellectual-property provisions, or material liability. These reviews are independent: prior use of a supplier does not replace review for a new data type or service scope.

## Prohibited circumvention

Requests may not be divided to avoid an approval threshold. Purchase cards may not be used for recurring software, cash equivalents, personal expenses, or services requiring a contract. A vendor sponsor may coordinate reviews but cannot grant technical access; account provisioning follows the Vendor and Contractor Access Policy.

## Emergency purchases and records

An incident commander and Finance approver may authorize an emergency purchase needed to contain an active operational or security incident. The requester must document why normal timing was unsafe, the maximum commitment, and the chosen supplier. Procurement and Legal must receive the record for retrospective review within two business days.

Contracts, approvals, invoices, security assessments, and exception records must be retained in the procurement system. Renewal owners must revalidate business need, supplier access, data handling, and budget before automatic renewal dates.
